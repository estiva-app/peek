# Activity indicator on each topic and team

A prototype on the `concept/activity-indicator` branch of Peek. It never merges. It draws the project's calls of 10 November **on Peek as it ships today**: Peek's own frame and rows, fed made-up content, with only this project's changes on top. Peek only; Ship and Leaf come later from the same rule.

**Start here:** [Topics, when both, blue wins](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--topics-blue-wins). Rest the pointer on a row with a dot, then move away: Widgets' orange goes, Release notes keeps its blue.

## Changed from today

- **A lit row opens a card when you rest the pointer on it**, as a Screener item does: what lit it, newest first. Each line is who, what (started a conversation, replied in your conversation, mentioned you, reacted 👍 to your message, wrote), the file it was on when it was on a listed file, the words, and how long ago. A click opens it where it was said. At most five lines; the rest are "5 more in For you". With nothing to list there is no dot and no card.
- **The card opens on the whole row, not on the dot alone.** Today a row with a "⋮" hides its dot while the pointer is on the row, so a card on the dot would vanish as you reach it.
- **Orange: someone reacted to your message**, in the place or on a file it lists. Leaving the card after it was open clears the orange; it never clears blue.
- **The same on every row:** a topic, a team's chat, a space, a topic a team leads, and a DM. [Teams](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--teams) · [People](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--people)
- **A team's name has no dot**: everything under it is always on screen with its own (the agent's call, yours to overrule).
- **An urgent DM with a reaction keeps the urgent mark**; its card lists both. The urgent mark and orange are the same colour today.
- **For you** is in the right-hand column, where a thread and a file's Overview open: *New* on top, then *Earlier*, which keeps what the hover cleared. It is not called Activity because a file's *Activity* already lists where else the file was mentioned (record activity-lists-only-mentions-from-elsewhere).
- **On a touch screen there is no hover**, so no card: opening the place clears its orange and what was said in it; blue on a listed file stays until that file is opened; what lit it is under the bell. Today a row with a "⋮" shows the "⋮" in place of its dot on a touch screen, so a phone shows no dot on a topic at all; the story draws the dot where a pointer screen does. [On a touch screen](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--touch)
- **The follow switch says what the topic shows and what lights it.** Not following: "Shows its own conversation. Nothing lights." Important: "Shows its own conversation. Lights for new conversations, replies in yours, and what names you on its files." All: "Shows its files' threads too. Lights for all of it." A team's chat lists no files, so its words say only what lights.

The card alone, each state without hovering: [blue only](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-blue-only) · [orange only](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-orange-only) · [both](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-both) · [urgent DM](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-urgent-dm) · [long](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-long).

**Rougher than the app:** opening a place shows a stand-in for its conversation; a line's click shows a toast in place of opening the message; the rail's Desk does not open; emoji may draw as boxes where the browser has no emoji font.
