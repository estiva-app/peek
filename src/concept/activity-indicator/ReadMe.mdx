# Activity indicator on each topic and team

A prototype on the `concept/activity-indicator` branch of Peek. It never merges. It draws the project's calls of 10 November **on Peek as it ships today**: Peek's own frame and rows, fed made-up content, with only this project's changes on top. Peek only; Ship and Leaf come later from the same rule.

**Click through:** every story is in the sidebar under *Prototype / Activity indicator / Screens*. Start with [Topics: the dot explains itself](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--topics-blue-wins): rest the pointer on a row with a dot, then move away (Widgets' orange goes, Release notes keeps its blue). Click a row to open it, then rest the pointer on the dot beside its title for its whole list.

## Decided on the first look (Miky, 10 Nov)

- A row with both new things to read and a reaction to you draws **one blue dot**; its card lists the reaction in orange.
- **The card in Topics or Teams shows only what is new.** With nothing new there is no dot and no card.
- **A topic's or a team's own page shows the whole list**, new and earlier, even with nothing new.
- **That list is behind a dot beside the page's title**, as the navigation prototype of 29 October drew it (estiva-ui `concept/cross-app-navigation`), not in the right-hand column.

## Changed from today

- **A lit row opens a card when you rest the pointer on it**, as a Screener item does: what is new, newest first. Each line is who, what (started a conversation, replied in your conversation, mentioned you, reacted 👍 to your message, wrote), the file it was on when it was on a listed file, the words, and how long ago. A click opens it where it was said. At most five lines; more than that ends with "5 more", which opens the place.
- **The card opens on the whole row, not on the dot alone.** Today a row with a "⋮" hides its dot while the pointer is on the row, so a card on the dot would vanish as you reach it.
- **Orange: someone reacted to your message**, in the place or on a file it lists. Leaving the card after it was open clears the orange; it never clears blue.
- **The same on every row:** a topic, a team's chat, a space, a topic a team leads, and a DM. [Teams](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--teams) · [People](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--people)
- **A team's name has no dot**: everything under it is always on screen with its own (the agent's call, yours to overrule). It opens the team's page, whose title dot lists everything under the team, each line saying where. [A team's page](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--page-team)
- **An urgent DM with a reaction keeps the urgent mark**; its card lists both. The urgent mark and orange are the same colour today.
- **A dot beside a page's title**: blue with something new to read, orange with only a reaction to you, a quiet ring with nothing new. Resting the pointer on it shows the place's whole list: *New* on top, then *Earlier*, which keeps what a hover cleared. It has no name on screen; a file's *Activity* in its Overview still means where else the file was mentioned (record activity-lists-only-mentions-from-elsewhere). [After the hover](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--page-after-the-hover) · [Long](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--page-long) · [Nothing yet](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--page-empty)
- **On a touch screen there is no hover**, so no card on the row: opening the place clears its orange and what was said in it; blue on a listed file stays until that file is opened; tapping the dot beside the title opens its list. Today a row with a "⋮" shows the "⋮" in place of its dot on a touch screen, so a phone shows no dot on a topic at all; the story draws the dot where a pointer screen does. [On a touch screen](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--touch)
- **The follow switch says what the topic shows and what lights it.** Not following: "Shows its own conversation. Nothing lights." Important: "Shows its own conversation. Lights for new conversations, replies in yours, and what names you on its files." All: "Shows its files' threads too. Lights for all of it." A team's chat lists no files, so its words say only what lights. [On a topic](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--switch-new-words) · [On a team's chat](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--switch-chat) · [Today](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--switch-today)

The card alone, each state without hovering: [blue only](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-blue-only) · [orange only](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-orange-only) · [both](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-both) · [urgent DM](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-urgent-dm) · [long](https://concept-activity-indicator.estiva-peek-storybook.pages.dev/?path=/story/prototype-activity-indicator-screens--card-long).

## New here

| New here | What it could have been |
| --- | --- |
| The orange dot | The kit's UnreadDot, which has one colour; extended with a second |
| Orange in the urgent mark's colour (the kit's warning colour) | A colour of its own, which the kit does not have; Katerina's to pick |
| The hover card's lines (face, name, what, where, when) | The Screener's preview card, which shows a message and its replies |
| The dot beside a page's title, and its list | The navigation prototype's Activity dot (29 Oct), never built; a file's Activity in its Overview lists mentions from elsewhere |

Reused as they are: the kit's PreviewCard for both hovers, Peek's TreeRow, NavItem and PersonItem rows, and the kit's follow switch with its words changed.

**Rougher than the app:** opening a place shows a stand-in for its conversation or page; a line's click shows a toast in place of opening the message; the rail's Desk does not open; emoji may draw as boxes where the browser has no emoji font.
