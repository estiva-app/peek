# RailListColumn

A page's list column that closes with the rail.

**Seen in** — *Pages/In the shell* (the People and Desk stories).

## When

- The list column of a page inside `PeekFrame`: Desk and People.

## When not

- Outside the frame. Use **ListColumn** directly, which has no rail to follow.

## How

```tsx
import { RailListColumn } from '@/layouts/RailListColumn'

<RailListColumn title="People">...rows...</RailListColumn>
```

- Takes the kit's `ListColumnProps` except `collapsed`.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** reading whether the rail is closed, from where `PeekFrame` provides it, and passing that as `collapsed`.
- **The look and the behaviour are the kit's ListColumn.** See ListColumn in the estiva-ui Storybook (Page structure / ListColumn).
