# SummaryCount

The warning under a summary field when the summary has grown long.

**Seen in** — *Topics/StartTopicDialog* (the ALongSummary story).

## When

- Under a Summary field. It draws nothing until the text is past the limit (about 240 characters).

## When not

- To stop the person. It warns and never refuses.
- For any other field's help. Use **FieldLine**.

## How

```tsx
import { SummaryCount } from '@/components/SummaryField'

<SummaryCount text={summary} />
```

- `text` is the only prop. `SummaryField` draws the field with it; Details uses it as its draft note.

## What it owns

- **The words:** the count and "A summary reads best in one or two sentences, under 240."
- **The look is the kit's FieldLine** in its warning tone. See FieldLine in the estiva-ui Storybook (Forms / Field line).
