# BoxReference

A `nostr:` reference or an app link inside the box, drawn as the kit's chip a
sent message draws, wherever it sits: in the box every link is a chip while it
is written, alone on its line too.

<Canvas of={ConversationBoxStories.WithAnIssue} />

**Seen in** — *Writing & text/Composer/ConversationBox* → **With An Issue**
(in a sentence) and **An Issue Alone On Its Line** (alone on its line, still a
chip).

## When

- As the `referenceView` the package's `CommentBox` draws a reference with —
  handed to `keepCommentBoxWarm` once as Peek starts, and used by every
  **ConversationBox**.

## When not

- In a sent message → `MessageChip` in a sentence, `MessageRow` alone on its
  line, both through `MessageBody`; there a click follows the link and hovering
  shows its card.

## How

```tsx
import { keepCommentBoxWarm } from '@estiva-app/ui/editor'
import { BOX_EXTENSIONS, BoxReference } from '@/components/ui/ConversationBox'

keepCommentBoxWarm({ extensions: BOX_EXTENSIONS, referenceView: BoxReference })
```

## What it owns

- **One chip in every box**: the kit's `LinkChip` — its icon, its key, its
  title cut at 40 characters and its status as a mark — for a pasted link and a
  `[` pick alike (Katerina, 30 Oct). A person is their name's chip.
- **Reading the object**: from what a story or a test supplied, else from the
  relay, as the sent message's chip reads it; an app link not answered yet
  reads as the URL it was written as.
- **No way out of the draft**: a click on the chip does not follow it, and
  hovering it shows no card (`preview={false}`).
- **Never a row or a card in the box**: no `LinkPlaces` in `BOX_EXTENSIONS`
  (the kit's `LinkPlaces` page, When not).
