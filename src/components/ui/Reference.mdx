# Reference

A `nostr:` pointer drawn where it was written, as a chip: a person's name, or
another app's object by its title and status, or the pointer's own short form
when nothing could be looked up.

**Seen in** — *Writing & text/MessageBody* → **Inline Reference**, and
*Writing & text/Composer/ConversationBox* → **With An Issue**, where the same chip
sits in the text being written.

## When

- A pointer in the middle of a sentence, in a sent message: `MessageBody` draws
  it through this.
- The same pointer while it is being written, in the composer (UIG-40): the
  editor's `objectMention` draws it through this with `link={false}`, so what is
  written looks like what is sent.

## When not

- A pointer alone on its line in a sent message. That is a card below the text,
  `ForeignObjectWidget`, and `MessageBody` chooses between the two.
- A person picked from the `@` menu, or a topic from the `[` menu, while
  writing: those are the editor's own chips (`mention`, `topicMention`), which
  know their label without looking anything up.
- A link to a web page. That is a link, not a reference.

## How

```tsx
import { Reference } from '@/components/ui/Reference'

<Reference uri={uri} object={resolved[uri]} />
<Reference uri={uri} object={resolved[uri]} link={false} />
```

- **It never looks anything up.** The caller resolves the pointers and hands in
  the result: `MessageBody` once for a whole body, the composer's chip for
  itself. `undefined` is still looking, `null` is nothing found; both draw the
  short form.
- `link={false}` in an editor: a click while writing is not a way out of the
  draft, and the editor draws every link blue and underlined.

## What it owns

- **What a pointer is called.** A person's name from the directory; an object's
  title as its app's manifest declares it (a person's name where the title is a
  key, a message's opening words); the pointer's short form otherwise, never a
  blank.
- **Where a click goes,** unless `link={false}`: a person to their DM, a topic
  to the topic, a message to its conversation, another app's object to that app
  in a new tab.
- **The chip's size.** One line, the title cut at 24 characters' width with the
  whole of it on hover, the status after it in its own colour.
