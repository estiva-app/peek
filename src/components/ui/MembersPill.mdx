# MembersPill

Who is in this conversation: up to three faces and the total, in a small
outlined pill.

**Seen in** — *Messages/ConversationHeader* and *Messages/ThreadPanel*. Those
are both the places it is used, which is why it has no story of its own.

## When

- In the header of anything with members: a topic, a group, a huddle.
- With `onClick` when the membership can be opened or changed.

## When not

- Without `onClick` as a way of hiding it. It stays visible and goes
  **disabled**, which is deliberate: a huddle's membership is fixed at creation,
  and the reader still needs to see who is in it.
- For a single person. At `total === 0` it draws nothing, but a one-person
  conversation wants that person's name, not a pill counting to one.

## How

```tsx
import { MembersPill } from '@/components/ui/MembersPill'

<MembersPill members={names} count={memberCount} onClick={openMembers} />
```

- `members` is the names in order; at most three faces are drawn.
- `count` is the number beside them, defaulting to how many names there are —
  pass it when you have more members than names loaded.

## What it owns

- **Being one pill rather than two.** `ConversationHeader` drew this and
  `ThreadPanel` re-typed it, down to the padding, and the copy had drifted
  (COMPONENTS-PEEK F12). Both call this now.
- **Faces that are the size they say they are.** The re-typed copy stacked its
  own avatars in `overflow-hidden border-2` boxes, which clips the face and the
  right edge of the initials, and takes the border out of the inside — a 24px
  avatar showed 20px of face. The package's `AvatarGroup` draws its ring as a
  shadow, outside the face, so 24 means 24.
- **Disabled rather than a different element** when there is nothing to press,
  so one set of pixels covers both cases.
- **Drawing nothing at zero**, so no call site needs that guard.

## Props

| | |
| --- | --- |
| `members` | required — names in order, up to three faces drawn |
| `count` | the total shown; defaults to `members.length` |
| `onClick` | omit it and the pill is disabled, not hidden |
| `className` | passed through |
