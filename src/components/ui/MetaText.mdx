# MetaText

The small figure beside a name or a count: a message's time, a thread entry's
time, a topic's open and resolved counts. In Signal it is the mono micro-type
with even digits.

<Canvas of={MetaTextStories.Default} />

## When

- A message's time, beside its author: every card, the pinned message.
- A count that sits in a line of meta: a topic's "6 open · 0 resolved".
- At `size="chip"`, beside a small name: the thread panel's entries, the
  Screener's preview.

## When not

- A number that is the point of the row — an unread count, a badge. That is a
  part of its own, not meta.
- A label over a group: that is `SectionLabel`.

## How

```tsx
import { MetaText } from '@/components/ui/MetaText'

<MetaText>{timestamp}</MetaText>
<MetaText tone="secondary">{openCount} open</MetaText>
<MetaText size="chip" className="shrink-0">{time}</MetaText>
```

- `tone`: `muted` for a time (the default); `secondary` and `success` for the
  open and resolved counts.
- `className` places it — `shrink-0`, `contents` — and nothing else.

## What it owns

- **The look, written once.** It was typed by hand in nine places (UIG-25);
  a change to how a time looks is one edit here.
- **One line.** It never wraps.
- **The Signal voice** — mono, 10px, wide tracking, even digits — through
  `signal:` variants, never at a call site.
