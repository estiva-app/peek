# ForeignObjectCard

An item from another app, drawn as a bordered card: its title, its status, the
people on it, and the controls to change it.

<Canvas of={ForeignObjectStories.Assigned} />

**Nothing here knows it is looking at Ship.** Every field, every control and
every word comes from the object's manifest. Peek supplies the widgets; the
owning app supplies the meaning.

## When

- Inside a message that references another app's object.
- When you already **have** the object. This one draws; `ForeignObjectWidget`
  is the one that fetches.

## When not

- For a project that is the subject of a whole column. The topic sidebar draws
  its own layout on purpose: a project you are looking *at* wants a properties
  list and a ticket table, and a reference sitting inside a message wants a line
  (PEEK-24).
- As a general card. That is the package's `Card`.

<Canvas of={ForeignObjectStories.ReadOnly} />

## How

```tsx
import { ForeignObjectCard } from '@/components/ui/ForeignObjectWidget'

<ForeignObjectCard object={resolved} onAct={act} />
```

## What it owns

- **The split from fetching.** Two concerns, two components, so a story can draw
  a card without a relay.
- **The "Open in …" link.** Solid rather than dotted — one look for "open it
  elsewhere" in both apps (Katerina, 13 September) — at the size and tone of the
  words around it (UIG-9), and `noopener`, because that URL is one a stranger
  published into a shared relay.
- **Drawing no link at all when the object carries no `openUrl`.**
