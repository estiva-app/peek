# MessageReactions

The reactions under a message or a reply: the package's `ReactionRow`, the same
row Ship and Leaf draw, with what only Peek knows around it.

**Seen in** — *Messages/ConversationCard* → **With reactions**, and
*Messages/ReplyCard*. Those are both the places it is used.

## When

- Under a message or a reply that somebody has reacted to. Nobody has: it draws
  nothing, and React stays in the card's hover strip.
- With `onToggle` where a reaction can be written. Without it (an archived file,
  a card nothing writes from) the pills only show and there is no add button.
- With `refused` after the relay refused this message's last reaction: "Not
  reacted: …" under the row, never a toast.

## When not

- Drawing pills one by one, or importing `Reaction` from `@estiva-app/ui`: the
  row is what keeps Peek, Ship and Leaf the same.
- Offering the emoji anywhere else: the five and their words are
  `@estiva-app/conversation`'s `REACTION_EMOJI`, through `REACTION_OPTIONS`.

## How

```tsx
import { MessageReactions } from '@/components/ui/MessageReactions'

<MessageReactions reactions={reactions} refused={refusedReaction} onToggle={readOnly ? undefined : toggle} />
```

- `reactions` is each emoji and who chose it by name, in reaction order, the
  reader as `CURRENT_USER_NAME`; their pills are lit and the hover says "you".

## What it owns

- Peek's indent under the message, its reader, and the refusal line.
- Stopping a click inside the row (its picker opens in a portal) from opening
  the message's thread.
