# ConversationBox

Where a message or a reply is written in Peek.

**Seen in** — No story: it needs the relay to send. *Pages/In the shell* (FileArchived) draws its closed form, `ClosedBox`.

## When

- Under a conversation (`kind="conversation"`) or in a thread (`kind="reply"`).

## When not

- To change a message already sent. That is `EditBox`.
- Where nothing can be written. Use `ClosedBox`, or pass `closed`.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationBox } from '@/components/ui/ConversationBox'

<ConversationBox kind="reply" onSend={send} draftKey={draftKey} contextLabel="Topic" here={address} />
```

- `onSend` gets the text, files, highlight, urgent names and cards, and answers whether the relay took it. `draftKey` keeps unsent text.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** its drafts, the people `@` offers, the `[` list, uploads, the card shown under the box, and its Resolve row.
- **The look and the behaviour are the kit's CommentBox.** See CommentBox in the estiva-ui Storybook (Writing / CommentBox).
