# ConversationBox

Peek's comment box: the package's `CommentBox`, the one box every app writes a
comment, a reply, a DM and a Folder's chat line in (PEEKM-3), with what only
Peek knows — its people, its `/` rows, its upload, its drafts and its send.

<Canvas of={ConversationBoxStories.Default} />

**Seen in** — its stories: **Default**, **Reply**, **With An Issue**, **An
Issue Alone On Its Line**, **Refused** and **Archived**; under a topic's or a
file's messages, in an open thread, in a DM and in a Folder's chat.

## When

- Wherever a person writes a new message or a reply in Peek: a topic, a file's
  conversation, a thread, a DM, a Folder's chat.

## When not

- Editing a sent message → the edit boxes in `ConversationCard` and
  `ReplyCard`, until the project's edit issue moves them onto the box.
- Nothing can be written (an archived file) → **ClosedBox**, the line in the
  box's place.
- In another app → its own wrapper of `CommentBox` (Ship's `ConversationBox`,
  Leaf's), never this one.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationBox } from '@/components/ui/ConversationBox'

<ConversationBox
  kind="reply"
  onSend={(payload, away) => send(payload, away)}
  draftKey={draftKeys.object(file.address)}
  contextLabel={title}
  here={file.address}
/>
```

## What it owns

- **Shown at once** (Decided 2026-10-17): the box empties as soon as Send is
  pressed; a refusal puts the words back with a line under the box saying why,
  in plain words (`notSentReason`) — no toast. A refusal that arrives after the
  person has left the box is said in a note instead.
- **Peek's `/` rows**: Resolve and the five Highlights, after the package's
  Upload, Mention, Urgent and Reference.
- **The draft**, kept under `draftKey` until it is sent.
- **The launcher's target**: the box last used is the one the command launcher
  writes into.
