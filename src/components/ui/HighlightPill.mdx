# HighlightPill

The small label naming a message's highlight — Insight, Question, Decision —
with its colour square in front.

<Canvas of={HighlightPillStories.Default} />

<Canvas of={HighlightPillStories.AllTypes} />

## When

- On a message that carries a highlight: in a conversation card, in a reply, and
  on the pinned message at the top of a topic.

## When not

- In a menu, where the name is already written beside it. There you want the
  square alone — `HighlightSwatch`, in this same file.
- To *set* a highlight. This says what one is; `HighlightButton` is what changes
  it.

## How

```tsx
import { HighlightPill } from '@/components/ui/HighlightPill'

<HighlightPill type={message.highlightType} />
```

- `type` is a `HighlightType`. There is no "none" — a message without a
  highlight draws no pill, and that is the caller's condition, not this one's.

## What it owns

- **The label and the colour, both looked up rather than passed.** `HIGHLIGHT_META`
  gives the word and `HIGHLIGHT_FILL_CLASSES` the square's fill, so no call site
  writes either, and renaming a highlight changes one map.
- **Surviving a tight row.** `whitespace-nowrap` and `shrink-0`: a pill in a
  conversation header is the thing that would otherwise be squeezed into two
  lines or clipped.
- **The Signal voice** for the word — mono, small, semibold, wide tracking —
  applied through `signal:` variants rather than at each call site.
