# MessageBody

The text of a message or reply, with its mentions, links and the cards its author chose.

**Seen in** — No story: it reads links from the relay. It is drawn inside the kit's ConversationCard, whose stories are in the estiva-ui Storybook.

## When

- Inside every card that shows a message: `ConversationCard`, `CommentRow`, the Screener preview.

## When not

- For text you are writing. That is `ConversationBox`.
- For plain formatted text with no links to resolve. Use **RichText**.

## How

```tsx
import { MessageBody } from '@/components/ui/MessageBody'

<MessageBody body={text} parts={parts} embeds={embeds} here={address} readOnly={archived} />
```

- `preview` shows links as chips only. `readOnly` shows card values as words. `onRemoveCard` adds the author's cross.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** `[Title]` and `@` chips, a lookup of the links once per body, the parts and cards under it, the limits (6 parts, 8 links), and where each link lands.
- **The look is the kit's RichText, LinkChip, LinkRow and AttachedCard.** See RichText in the estiva-ui Storybook (Writing / RichText).
