# ConfirmDelete

The question asked before a file is deleted from its menu.

**Seen in** — No story: it deletes a real file, so it is only reached from a file's menu in the app.

## When

- Delete is chosen from a topic's or a file's menu. A message and a reply are asked by the kit's card instead.

## When not

- To ask anything else. Use **ConfirmDialog** with your own words.
- To archive. Pass `onArchiveInstead` to offer it as the safer answer here.

## How

```tsx
import { ConfirmDelete } from '@/components/ConfirmDelete'

<ConfirmDelete onConfirm={deleteFile} onClose={close} onArchiveInstead={canArchive ? archive : undefined} />
```

- `onConfirm` may be async. `onArchiveInstead` adds the "Archive instead" answer when given.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** the words. The title "Delete?", and the two lines saying Peek asks the relay, the relay decides, and other apps keep their copies.
- **The look and the behaviour are the kit's ConfirmDialog**: Escape and the cross cancel, the backdrop does not. See ConfirmDialog in the estiva-ui Storybook (Menus & windows / ConfirmDialog).
