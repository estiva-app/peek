# ConversationCard

One message in a file's conversation or a direct message, as Peek shows it.

**Seen in** — No story: it draws the kit's ConversationCard, whose stories are in the estiva-ui Storybook, and it needs the relay.

## When

- Every message and reply in Peek's own conversations.

## When not

- For another app's comment. That is `CommentRow`.
- For a reply in another app's thread. Use **ReplyCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard } from '@/components/ConversationCard'

<ConversationCard authorName="Ada" timestamp="2m" body={text} messageId={id} replyCount={2} onReply={openThread} />
```

- Props are Peek's: `authorName`, `timestamp`, `body`, `reactions`, `files`, `embeds`, `parts`, and the `on...Change` handlers.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** reactions, edits, resolve and delete shown at once and handed to the relay; an edit the relay refused is opened again; Peek's edit box and its words ("message", "Utilities").
- **The look and the behaviour are the kit's ConversationCard.** See ConversationCard in the estiva-ui Storybook (Conversation / ConversationCard).
