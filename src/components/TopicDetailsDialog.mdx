# TopicDetailsDialog

Everything a topic is, and the one place its name can be changed (PEE-2).

<Canvas of={TopicDetailsStories.CanRename} />

## When

- From the ⋯ menu, wherever it is opened from — the sidebar row or the
  conversation header.
- For any topic, whether or not this reader may rename it.

## When not

- As a rename prompt. Renaming lives here, but the dialog has to be worth
  opening when you can change **nothing** — which is what the members, the
  project and the history are for. Peek had nowhere showing who started a topic,
  who is in it, what project it is attached to, or what has happened to it.
- In the header title. That was the alternative and it fails on two counts —
  see below.

<Canvas of={TopicDetailsStories.ReadOnly} />

## Why renaming is here and not in the header title

**Permission.** Only the topic's creator may rename it: the relay grants a
`kind:9002` name change to a channel's owner or admin. A details row can simply
be a *label* for everybody else. A header title would have to be an input that
refuses to be typed in.

**Failure.** When the relay says no, a dialog has somewhere to put the reason. A
title in a header has nowhere at all (Katerina, 19 August 2026).

## How

```tsx
import { TopicDetailsDialogView } from '@/components/TopicDetailsDialog'

<TopicDetailsDialogView
  details={details}
  activity={activity}
  folderProject={project}
  onRename={rename}
  onClose={close}
/>
```

- `onRename` resolves to `null` on success, or to an outcome carrying a message
  — which is how the same dialog says both "the relay refused" and "renamed, but
  not announced".

## What it owns

- **The difference between a refusal and a partial success.** Renamed-but-not-
  announced is not a failure and must not look like one: the topic *was*
  renamed, the relay just was not told, so the editor closes and the note is a
  warning rather than an error. Red under a name that had visibly changed read
  as "this failed" (Katerina, 20 August).
- **A label rather than a disabled field** for everybody who cannot rename. A
  greyed-out input says "you could type here but we won't let you" and invites
  somebody to try; a label just says what the name is.
- **Showing a project's work the same way the Files panel does**, through
  `ProjectTickets` — the same accordion, counter, bar and rows, because two
  renderings of one thing is how they drift, and these two are one click apart.
