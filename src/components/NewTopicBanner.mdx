# NewTopicBanner

The strip above the composer of a direct message that has no messages yet —
"this is the beginning of your conversation with…".

<Canvas of={NewTopicBannerStories.DmBeginning} />

## When

- Above the composer, when a direct message has nothing in it yet.

## When not

- For an empty *list* — a topic with no replies, a folder with nothing in it.
  That is `EmptyState`. This one is specifically the head of a conversation that
  is about to have its first message written into it.
- To announce anything else. It is the package's `Banner` in its `info` tone
  (Katerina, 18 September); a warning or an error is that component directly,
  with its own tone.

## How

```tsx
import { NewTopicBanner } from '@/components/NewTopicBanner'

<NewTopicBanner name={partnerName} />
```

- `name` is the other person's name.

## What it owns

- **The sentence**, and the emphasis on the name, so it reads as the subject of
  the sentence rather than as part of it.
- Its topic version, with **Invite members**, had no caller and went (audit D8,
  2 November).
