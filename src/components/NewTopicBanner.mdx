# NewTopicBanner

The strip above the composer of a conversation that has no messages yet — "this
is the beginning of…" — with an invitation to bring people in, where that makes
sense.

<Canvas of={NewTopicBannerStories.Default} />

## When

- Above the composer, when a topic or a direct message has nothing in it yet.
- `kind="topic"` for a topic or group, which gets **Invite members**.
- `kind="dm"` for a direct message, which does not.

<Canvas of={NewTopicBannerStories.DmBeginning} />

## When not

- For an empty *list* — a topic with no replies, a folder with nothing in it.
  That is `EmptyState`. This one is specifically the head of a conversation that
  is about to have its first message written into it.
- To announce anything else. It is the package's `Banner` in its `info` tone
  (Katerina, 18 September); a warning or an error is that component directly,
  with its own tone.

## How

```tsx
import { NewTopicBanner } from '@/components/NewTopicBanner'

<NewTopicBanner title={topic.title} onInviteMembers={openInvite} />
<NewTopicBanner title={partnerName} kind="dm" />
```

- `title` is the topic's title for a topic, and the other person's name for a
  direct message.

## What it owns

- **The two sentences, and which one applies.** "…your conversations in" for a
  topic, "…your conversation with" for a direct message — singular against
  plural, decided here rather than at the two call sites.
- **That a direct message gets no invite action.** You cannot invite a third
  person into a DM, so the action is not merely disabled, it is absent.
- **The emphasis on the name**, so the title reads as the subject of the
  sentence rather than as part of the sentence.
