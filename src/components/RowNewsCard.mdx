# RowNewsCard

What is new on a lit sidebar row, in the card that opens while the row is pointed at (ACT-3).

**Seen in** — No story: its lines are the row's own unread messages, read from the relay, so it is reached by resting on a lit topic, space, team chat or DM in the app.

## When

- A topic's or space's row in Topics or Teams, a team chat's row, or a DM's row in People, while it has a dot. Its lines are the ones that lit the dot.

## When not

- To preview a conversation in full. Use **ScreenerPreviewCard**.
- On a row with nothing new, or on the rows of a topic's files under the topic's row: pass `NavItem` no `preview`.

## How

```tsx
import { RowNewsCard } from '@/components/RowNewsCard'

<NavItem
  href={href}
  label={title}
  unread
  preview={lines.length ? <RowNewsCard items={lines} placeHref={href} place="topic" onOpen={remember} /> : undefined}
/>
```

- `items` are the row's `RowNewsItem`s, newest first, from the same hook that lights its dot.
- `place` is `topic`, `chat` or `dm`.
- `onOpen` runs what the row's own click runs before a line's page opens.

## What it owns

- One line per new message, at most five: who, what they did (the shared rule's reason, SPEC §11.8, in Peek's words), when, and the file title and words.
- "N more" past five, which opens the place.
- Where a line leads: a listed file's comment at its thread, a topic's message at its thread, and a team chat's or a DM's reply at its thread. A chat or DM message that answers nothing opens the place.
- The words of a topic's own messages and the titles of listed files, read once when the card opens (`useRowNewsDetails`). Nothing is read on a cold open.
