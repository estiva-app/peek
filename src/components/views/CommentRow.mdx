# CommentRow

One comment that belongs to another app, drawn in Peek.

**Seen in** — No story: it draws the kit's ConversationCard, whose stories are in the estiva-ui Storybook, and it needs a people directory.

## When

- A list of comments owned by another app, such as a file's comments. `CommentReply` in the same file is the reply.

## When not

- For a message in Peek's own conversation. That is `ConversationCard`, which can edit, resolve and react.

## How

```tsx
import { CommentRow } from '@/components/views/CommentRow'

<CommentRow comment={comment} people={people} replies={3} isNew selected onOpen={openThread} />
```

- `comment` has `id`, `author`, `body`, `createdAt` (Nostr seconds) and `deleted`. Without `onOpen` it offers nothing.

## What it owns

- **Peek's half:** who wrote it (a shortened key when there is no name), the short time, and the body through `MessageBody`. It offers no edit, resolve or reaction, because Peek cannot do them for another app's comment.
- **The look and the behaviour are the kit's ConversationCard.** See ConversationCard in the estiva-ui Storybook (Conversation / ConversationCard).
