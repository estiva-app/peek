# TeamChat

A team's chat: the messages with the team's `h` and no file named, with a composer, in the conversation view a DM uses, under a topic's header.

**Seen in** — No story: its messages, its follow and its Open work are read from and written to the relay. Its parts are the kit's **ConversationLayout** and **PageHeader**.

## When

- The Teams page, when a team's chat is opened from its row in Teams.
- The Desk's panel, when a team's chat is opened from a line or from Open work. Pass `openLineHere` so a line in its title dot's list opens in that panel.

## When not

- For a topic's or a file's conversation. Those are a file's page, **FilePage**.
- For a DM. A DM has its own page.

## How

```tsx
import { TeamChat } from '@/components/views/TeamChat'

<TeamChat team={team} />
<TeamChat key={team.id} team={team} openLineHere={openLine} />
```

- `team` is a row from `useTeams`: its `chatAddress`, `chat.in` and `archived` decide the follow, the bell and Add to Open work.
- A link may name a thread (`?thread=<root>`). It opens beside the messages and is scrolled into view where it was said.

## What it owns

- **The header:** `#` before the chat's name, the title dot, Who follows (people only, no Add or Join), the bell while you are in it, **OpenWorkButton** for someone in the chat, and a ⋮ with Copy link alone.
- **Reading:** opening the chat marks it read, as opening a DM does.
- **Ctrl/⌘+O:** adds the chat to Open work, or takes it out.
- **An archived team's chat:** read-only, with no Add to Open work.
