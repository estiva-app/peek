# Peek

The Storybook for **Peek** — the app's own screens, and the parts they are built
from.

**The shared parts are not here.** `Button`, `Avatar`, `Chip`, `Tooltip`,
`Dialog` and the rest belong to `@estiva-app/ui`, and their stories live in that
package's Storybook — `npm run storybook` in `estiva-ui`,
[http://localhost:6008](http://localhost:6008). Peek forwards twenty of them
under its own names, and a forward gets no story here: a second story of the
same part is a second thing to keep true.

What *is* here is Peek's own — the conversation surface, topics, huddles, the
screener and the desk, the frame, and the parts built for them.

## One theme

Peek ships one theme, **Signal**, and cannot switch it at runtime. So there is
no theme picker. A component seen in any other theme would be a component in a
state nobody can reach.

Checking a *shared* part across themes is the package's Storybook's job, and it
has the picker. (Katerina, 11 September 2026 — D58.)

folder it happens to sit in.

## How a story is written here

- **Beside its component**, as `X.stories.tsx`. CSF3.
- **The first story is the picture.** The ordinary state, never a spinner —
  everything that links to a component links to its first story.
- **One story per state**, not per prop. A person reads states; the props table
  already lists props.
- **No real data.** Everything a story shows is invented for it.
- **Peek's use, not the package's.** A part that wraps something from
  `@estiva-app/ui` is shown in its place — inside the row, the card or the panel
  it belongs to — rather than documented a second time.

## What every story is already inside

The preview mounts what the app mounts, so a component deep in a tree finds what
it reaches for:

```
DebugProvider → PeekDataProvider → TopicStoreProvider
```

Without them, 67 of 316 stories were silent error pages — measured, not
guessed, on 1 September 2026. They are coming back out one story at a time, as
each story learns to take props instead.

`VITE_CONVEX_URL` is pinned to empty, so the data seam takes its static path
rather than subscribing to queries that never resolve without a signed-in
session.
