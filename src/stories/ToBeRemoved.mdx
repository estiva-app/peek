# To be removed

Stories that are on their way out wait here until they are deleted, instead of
disappearing inside somebody else's pull request. Katerina's idea, 22 September
2026: a thing waiting to go should be visible, not quietly rotting where it is.

**Three different things wait here, and the table says which.** Most are
components that stay — the app still draws them, and only the *story* is
parked, because Katerina wanted to look at the picture again before deciding.
One, `JoinTopicBanner`, is dead code: nothing in the app uses it, and it is
here to be **deleted**, not to be reconsidered. And four more are unused, and
wait for her to keep or delete each one from its picture (26 September).

The distinction matters when you clear this heading. Parking a story changes
nothing a person can see; deleting the component changes the app. Read the
**Why** column before you act on a row.

## The rule

Nothing goes under this heading without **a ticket number and the date it went
in**, both written in the row below. A heading with no rule becomes a junk
drawer, and a junk drawer is somewhere to lose things rather than somewhere to
find them.

It sorts last, under everything of Peek's own.

## One cost, flagged rather than buried

Parking `AttachmentChips` takes away the **only** picture anywhere of three
things: a file part-way through uploading, a file that failed, and the line
*"Other apps won't see this"*.

Those three states have not been drawn anywhere else. They should come back
inside the `ComposeBox` story rather than stay lost — that is not done yet.

## Deleted outright, not parked

`Layouts/App` and its `PeekApp` harness went on 22 September rather than waiting
here, because Katerina asked for them to go now. They were the whole running app
on three routes — Desk, Topics and People — fed by the invented data, which is
the thing being got rid of; and being live, each could only ever show the one
state the seed data put it in.

Peek therefore has **no page-level stories at all** until Ship's shape replaces
them: a presentational View fed a fixture, dropped into the shell, with a
loading state beside it. That work is in this same pull request.

`EditedMarker`'s story went on 23 September, and the component did not. Katerina
ruled that a standalone primitive showing three words beside a time says nothing
on an empty canvas: the marker is drawn where it means something, in
*Messages/ConversationCard* → **Edited** and *Messages/ReplyCard* → **Edited**,
and its rule page points at both. The same ruling ended its candidacy for the
package.
